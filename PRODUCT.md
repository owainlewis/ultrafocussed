# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

React, TypeScript, and Vite. Implementation default recommended in conversation; no alternative stack was specified.

## Users

Solo users planning work across several days. Personal use is the v1 assumption accepted in the proposed design.

## Product Purpose

A minimalist weekly planner. Capture goals, connect daily tasks to them, assign a planning date, choose a main outcome, and complete work.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Goals and the daily planner are in scope. The planner has a Week view and a Month view over the same tasks and goals; the Month view shows every date with goals and their 90-day focus alongside. Goals hold an outcome, reason, optional target date, next 90-day focus, and completion. Tasks optionally link to a goal. Browser-local persistence, unscheduled tasks, dated tasks, completion, notes, reordering, and one main outcome per day. No accounts, cloud sync, or team features.

## Brand Commitments

Ultrafocussed. Minimalist layout, quiet neutral surfaces, one muted accent, no permanent navigation sidebar.

## Product Principles

Fast capture. Explicit choices about unfinished work. One task shared across every view. No productivity scores or artificial sample work.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Keyboard alternatives to dragging, visible focus, labelled controls, and responsive mobile planning.
